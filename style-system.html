A mix of two directions. From OUP: structure and discipline — the Oxford-blue anchor, the navy-box logotype, generously rounded navy cards, and an editorial rhythm. From Oxford Tutors: warmth — a soft ivory ground rather than stark white, and restrained antique gold for fine accents. Studied from the Press, but distinctly OT, not a copy. 03 â€” Headings & body in context

How a column of text reads.

Serif for the things a reader lingers on; sans for the running text and every control. Measure is held near 62 characters.

A boutique practice grows quietly. For twenty-one years Oxford Tutors has worked with a small number of families at a time, by referral, never by advertising — and that is deliberate. The work is a relationship measured in years, not a package of hours.

What a family finds here is judgment: the right focus, the right mentors, and an honest account of whether and how we can help. The section rules, the restrained gold, and the unhurried spacing are all in service of that tone — considered, exact, and calm.
