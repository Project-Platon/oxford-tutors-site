About

A practice built around one person’s judgment.

The most durable trust in this work is a real, credentialed person. Oxford Tutors has been directed by its founder since 2005, and the mentors who do the work are chosen by hand.

EG

The Founder

Elisabeth Gray

Founder & Director

Raised by a behavioral developmental pediatrician, Elisabeth grew up around the full range of how children learn, through her mother’s practice. At Oxford University she found the one-on-one tutorial system, where careful questioning replaced rote memorization and taught her to think for herself. In 2005 she founded Oxford Tutors to bring that approach to students in the United States, from high-achievers seeking deeper challenge to complex learners navigating systems that were not built for them.

For more than two decades she has designed bespoke education for students across the globe. Today she serves as the firm’s intellectual architect: refining how the practice teaches, building clinical and school partnerships, and mentoring the next generation of Oxford Tutors educators.

  • Oxford University  BA (Hons), English Literature & Language
  • Yale University  M.Div, Theology & Literature
