About · The Oxford Tutors Method

Using the Socratic Method, we help every student recognize that everything they need to succeed is already within them.

The Socratic Method is question-based learning that activates critical thinking and creative problem solving rather than rewarding rote memorization. Ask most tutoring companies how they teach, and they hand you a program that was written before your child arrived. Ask here, and the first move is a question: who is this child? Everything follows from the answer. It is the oldest idea in teaching — one student and one tutor, thinking aloud together, for years.

What never changes

Five things we do, every time.

  1. 01

    Start with the student

    Before the first lesson, we work out how this particular mind moves. We meet it where it stands, not where a grade level says it ought to stand.

  2. 02

    One tutor, matched by hand

    A single senior tutor, chosen for the subject and for the temperament. Most families keep the same one for four to six years. That continuity is the point.

  3. 03

    Ask, do not tell

    This is the Socratic Method: question-based learning that activates critical thinking rather than rewarding rote memorization.

  4. 04

    Teach the whole student

    Focus, follow-through, the nerve to try: the things beneath the schoolwork belong inside the schoolwork, never bolted on beside it.

  5. 05

    Watch the growth, not the grade

    We track whether a student is becoming a genuine thinker. The scores rise as well. They are simply not the thing we aim at.

Why it fits learning differences

Teaching that bends to the child, rather than asking the child to bend.

A classroom teaches thirty children one way and hopes each one fits. We teach one. Nothing has to be standard, and nothing has to be forced. For a child who learns differently, this is not a nicety but the whole reason it works. The method fits learning differences precisely because it was never built for the average learner.

  • Personal by design
  • No standard mould to fit
  • Built for how they think

Who directs it

Elisabeth Gray

Founder & Director

Raised by a behavioral developmental pediatrician, Elisabeth grew up immersed in the wide range of learning types and abilities she encountered through her mother’s practice. At Oxford University, she discovered the one-on-one tutorial system, where the Socratic Method replaced rote memorization and activated her to think for herself. In 2005, she founded Oxford Tutors to bring that approach to students in the United States, from high-achievers seeking deeper challenge to complex learners navigating systems that weren’t built for them.

For more than two decades, Elisabeth has created bespoke education solutions across the globe, from California public schools to the Upper East Side, from elite prep schools to rural wilderness programs. Today, she serves as the firm’s intellectual architect: refining pedagogical frameworks, cultivating clinical and school partnerships, and mentoring the next generation of Oxford Tutors educators.
