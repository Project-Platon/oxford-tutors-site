Which of the twelve minds is yours?

Most assessments measure only the cost of how a mind works, and record nothing of what it yields. This one attends to the other half. Twenty-four questions place a mind among the twelve cognitive archetypes — the recognizable ways attention is aimed, information is shaped, the world is read, and the moment is met.

The questions take roughly four minutes, and no answer is the right one. What follows is a recognition rather than a diagnosis. Most people see themselves in more than one archetype, and that is the point.
