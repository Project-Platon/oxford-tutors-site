Visual direction proposal · One route

Oxford’s discipline, Oxford Tutors’ warmth.

A single direction for the website: study the visual language of Oxford University Press — its restraint, its navy, its classical type — and rebuild it as an Oxford Tutors identity, softened with warmth so it reads as a boutique practice, not a publishing house.

“The composure of a great press, the warmth of a family practice.”

Why this route

Studied, not copied.

From the Press

The Oxford blue anchor, a stacked serif-caps logotype, classical type, thin rules, generous space, and quiet confidence. Structure and restraint.

From Oxford Tutors

A warm ivory ground instead of stark white, and a restrained antique gold for fine accents. The warmth that signals a personal, bespoke relationship.

The result

Premium and trustworthy, but human. Clearly in the Oxford tradition, clearly its own brand — and video-first, so the tutors carry it.
