About · Our Story

Every student’s mind is different. Their education should be too.

A small, founder-led practice rests on one conviction: the teaching is built around the student, and never the student around the teaching. Begin there, and everything else follows.

Mission & Story

Built around one student at a time.

Raised by a behavioral developmental pediatrician, Elisabeth grew up immersed in the wide range of learning types and abilities she encountered through her mother’s practice. At Oxford University, she discovered the one-on-one tutorial system, where the Socratic Method replaced rote memorization and activated her to think for herself. In 2005, she founded Oxford Tutors to bring that approach to students in the United States, from high-achievers seeking deeper challenge to complex learners navigating systems that weren’t built for them.

For more than two decades, Elisabeth has created bespoke education solutions across the globe, from California public schools to the Upper East Side, from elite prep schools to rural wilderness programs. Today, she serves as the firm’s intellectual architect: refining pedagogical frameworks, cultivating clinical and school partnerships, and mentoring the next generation of Oxford Tutors educators.

So the practice begins the other way around. Before we teach anything, we learn the student: how the mind moves, what it carries, what stands in the way. We meet a child where the work actually sits, and not where a grade level insists it should. Then we build the teaching to fit.

The transcript is the smallest part of it. Confidence, the habits that make schoolwork possible, the settled sense that one’s own mind is worth trusting: these are the work, as fully as the essay or the equation. Attend to those, and the numbers tend to follow. They are the byproduct, and never the point.

What we believe

Four commitments, in every match.

Personal

Built for students who require more than a template. One student, one senior tutor, matched by mind and by temperament.

Holistic

We help every student recognize that everything they need to succeed is already within them.

Where you are

We start from the mind as it works, at the point it has reached, and we build forward from there.

Process, not score

This is optimization of potential, not remediation.

However a family finds us, the care is the same.

Some families come for a selective admission. Others come because a bright child is struggling in ways school keeps misreading. Most need both, and the practice is not right for everyone. We begin with the child, then bring in whatever the child requires.
