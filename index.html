Elisabeth Gray

Founder & Director

Raised by a behavioral developmental pediatrician, Elisabeth grew up immersed in the wide range of learning types and abilities she encountered through her mother’s practice. At Oxford University, she discovered the one-on-one tutorial system, where the Socratic Method replaced rote memorization and activated her to think for herself. In 2005, she founded Oxford Tutors to bring that approach to students in the United States, from high-achievers seeking deeper challenge to complex learners navigating systems that weren’t built for them.

For more than two decades, Elisabeth has created bespoke education solutions across the globe, from California public schools to the Upper East Side, from elite prep schools to rural wilderness programs. Today, she serves as the firm’s intellectual architect: refining pedagogical frameworks, cultivating clinical and school partnerships, and mentoring the next generation of Oxford Tutors educators.

How a partnership of years begins.

  1. 01

    Discovery Consultation

    A personal conversation with our team to understand your child’s profile, goals, and challenges. No automated scheduling — this is a concierge experience from day one.

  2. 02

    Personalized Strategy

    We build a roadmap that integrates academic development, testing, executive functioning, and admissions planning into a unified approach shaped by how your child actually learns and performs.

  3. 03

    Expert Team Assignment

    Your child is matched with a curated team, selected by academic goals, learning style, personality, and needs. Senior mentors oversee strategy; mentors deliver day-to-day instruction.

  4. 04

    Long-Term Partnership

    Most families stay with us for years, not months. We become part of your child’s academic world, adapting across milestones from middle school through college and beyond.
