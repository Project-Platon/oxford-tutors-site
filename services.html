Our Services

Comprehensive support at every stage of education.

Our services are integrated, never siloed, delivered in combination as part of a unified strategy tailored to each student’s mind, goals, and timeline.

Schedule a Discovery Call

We operate more like a boutique professional services firm than a conventional education provider. Families partner with us for expertise and strategic judgment, not pre-packaged instruction.

How we work

A relationship, not a package of sessions.

Your child is matched with one senior tutor who stays, held up by the wider team behind them. The work does not stop and start with the invoice. We plan for the long view, then we help in real time, as the decisions and the deadlines actually arrive.

As the child grows, the work grows with them: early tutoring gives way to deeper academic engagement, then to admissions, then to whatever follows that. Most families remain with us for years.

What the work can hold

College & Graduate Admissions
Long-range admissions strategy, from academic positioning and course selection to narrative development and essay coaching that preserves your child’s authentic voice. We craft stories, not just applications. Explore the Admissions path →
Learning Differences Support
Teaching built around how a bright, complicated child actually learns, coordinated with the school and the clinicians already involved. Explore the Learning Differences path →
Academic Mentorship
Deep, one-on-one coaching that focuses on how your child learns, developing metacognition, intellectual confidence, and mastery of challenging material. Designed to unleash your child’s potential. Explore Academic Mentorship →
Executive Functioning
Personalized systems for planning, prioritization, time management, and self-regulation, embedded within academic work rather than treated as a separate service. Practical strategies that stick. Explore Executive Functioning →
Test Preparation
Strategic test prep designed around how your child thinks and performs, not a one-size-fits-all curriculum. We custom design each student’s test prep plan in relation to their error analysis. We also use flexible session formats, including short high-frequency sessions that reduce burnout and build real confidence. SAT, ACT, SSAT, ISEE, and AP. Explore Test Preparation →
Educational Planning
Multi-year strategic planning that aligns academic choices, extracurriculars, and personal development toward your child’s long-range goals. We help families see the bigger picture and make confident decisions. Explore Educational Planning →
Family Consulting
Education is a family journey. We work closely with parents to develop aligned strategies, navigate school dynamics, and create supportive environments that complement our work with students. Explore Family Consulting →
