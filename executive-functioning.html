Overview

Executive functioning support helps students build systems for planning, prioritizing, starting work, managing time, and completing tasks. The work stays practical and connected to real academic demands.

Rather than treated as a separate service, it is embedded in the schoolwork a student is already doing.

What it includes

  • Organization
  • Planning
  • Time management
  • Study systems
  • Self-regulation
  • Task initiation
  • Parent alignment where useful
