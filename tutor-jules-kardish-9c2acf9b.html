Jules Kardish

Master of Philosophy in Education · Writing, Humanities & Executive Functioning

A humanities scholar, writer, and creative with a talent for helping students become thoughtful, confident writers and independent thinkers.

Biography

Jules Kardish is a humanities scholar, writer, artist, and creative educator specializing in humanities instruction, writing mentorship, and educational therapy. She earned a Master of Philosophy in Education from the University of Cambridge, where she received the Top Dissertation Award at Jesus College. She graduated magna cum laude from Harvard University with a joint degree in Arts, Film, and Visual Studies and Social Anthropology. While at Harvard, she was recognized as a John Harvard Scholar and received both the Gordon Parks Prize and the Rudolf Arnheim Prize for her interdisciplinary research and visual scholarship.

Drawing on a background in ethnographic research, visual storytelling, and critical theory, Jules helps students become thoughtful, confident writers and independent thinkers. She works closely with students to brainstorm passion projects, develop compelling analytical arguments, refine research projects, and approach complex texts with curiosity, precision, and originality.

Central to Jules’s philosophy is the belief that meaningful learning depends on the development of the skills that make learning possible. She incorporates principles of educational therapy into her work by helping students strengthen executive functioning, including organization, planning, task initiation, and time management. Rather than simply helping students complete assignments, she equips them with personalized strategies that foster independence, reduce academic overwhelm, and build confidence. Her goal is to help students understand how they learn best so they can become resilient, self-directed learners equipped for long-term success.

Teaching philosophy

My teaching philosophy is grounded in the belief that meaningful education is built on creativity, compassionate guidance, and dedicated pastoral care. I strive to create a learning environment where students feel safe to ask questions, embrace challenges, and discover confidence in their own abilities. Patience and empathy are at the heart of my approach, recognizing that every learner brings unique strengths, experiences, and ways of understanding the world.

Creativity plays a vital role in this process, encouraging students to think critically, solve problems with confidence, and engage deeply with their learning. By adapting my teaching to each student’s needs and interests, I seek to cultivate curiosity and intrinsic motivation, empowering learners to take ownership of their educational journey.
