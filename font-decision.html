Font decision · One choice needed

Which serif carries Oxford Tutors?

The build and the committed identity currently disagree on the headline serif. Same words, same layout, same navy — only the type changes. Pick one and I’ll make the whole build and the documents agree.

My rec

I recommend Libre Caslon. It was the committed choice, it’s the quintessential English book face (a real tie to the English-Literature founder and to Oxford), and it keeps us distinct from OUP rather than approximating their licensed font — which is exactly what the brief asked. Crimson Pro is the strong alternative if maximum OUP resemblance is the priority. Switching is a one-block CSS change either way.
