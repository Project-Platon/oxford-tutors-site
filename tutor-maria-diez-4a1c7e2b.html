Maria Díez

Educator & Teaching Artist · Academic Tutoring, Test Preparation & Spanish

A creative, empathetic tutor and teaching artist who helps students build confidence and self-advocacy as they master academic material.

Biography

Maria Díez has been a professional tutor since 2015 and has had a passion for education since high school. She began her career in arts education in Minnesota, working with students ages five to eighteen, before moving to New York to attend Columbia University, where she earned a Bachelor of Arts in Theatre and Dramatic Arts and was on the Dean’s List all four years. She also received the National Council of Teachers of English Achievement Award in Writing.

For more than a decade, Maria has worked with students from first grade through twelfth grade, as well as adult learners. She specializes in academic tutoring, standardized test preparation, Spanish and English as a Second Language instruction, and executive functioning support. Her test-preparation experience includes the SAT, ACT, ISEE, SHSAT, SSAT, Regents, and other state and New York City entrance exams. Her approach combines targeted content review with test-taking strategies, helping students recognize and demystify the patterns, question types, and expectations of each exam or class. She provides subject support for all humanities, sciences, and mathematics for elementary through high school, except for Advanced Placement Physics, Pre-Calculus, and Calculus.

Maria also provides college admissions and essay coaching, interview preparation, public speaking and podcast coaching, and individualized academic support. A native, fluent Spanish speaker, she works with students at all levels of Spanish proficiency, including Advanced Placement Spanish students.

Her students have gained admission to schools including Cornell, Brown, the University of Chicago, Syracuse, and Emory, and her Advanced Placement students have consistently earned fours and fives. She has also helped students move off academic probation and become more proactive in their learning and communication.

Teaching philosophy

I believe in the importance of understanding the student as a whole person. Academic challenges rarely exist in isolation; confidence, organization, stress, motivation, and competing responsibilities can all affect how a student learns. Building a strong relationship with each student is therefore central to my approach. I create an environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes, and working through challenges without judgment. There are no “dumb” questions, no single right way to learn, and “I don’t know” is always a good place to start.

I take time to understand how each student thinks, where they feel confident, and where they may need additional support. I adapt my explanations, activities, pacing, and strategies accordingly. Some students may benefit from visual models or hands-on practice, while others need to talk through a concept, see multiple examples, or break a challenging task into smaller steps. Learning, and learning how to learn, takes time, and I adjust my approach as a student grows. My goal is not simply to find a strategy that works once, but to help students discover how they learn best and what tools they can use whenever they see fit.

I provide regular updates about a student’s progress and next steps, and I am always happy to provide homework when appropriate. I also encourage students to build strong relationships with their teachers and develop the confidence to ask questions and advocate for themselves.

I believe students thrive when they feel supported, understood, and capable. Academic success is about more than mastering material; it also requires organization, resilience, confidence, self-advocacy, and the ability to learn from setbacks. Whether that means developing better study habits, finding organizational tools, or practicing mindfulness, I help students identify the tools that work for them. My goal is to help students become confident and curious learners who feel like they are putting their best foot forward.
