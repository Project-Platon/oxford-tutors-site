Palmer Manes

College Admissions Advisor & Mentor · Admissions Essay Writing

A Stanford graduate who prizes an individualized, humanistic approach to the college essay.

Biography

I’m Palmer Manes, a graduate of Stanford University. At Stanford, I earned a Bachelor of Arts with Honors in Religious Studies, and my honors thesis won the Bender Family Jewish Studies Undergraduate Award.

I have tutored since 2021, focusing on college admissions essay tutoring since 2022. I’m passionate about helping students develop the writing craft tools and techniques they need to produce compelling and effective prose. My focus is helping my students uncover and tell a story that reflects who they are, and I firmly believe that, when it comes to the process of writing college essays, no fancy slogan or algorithm can replace an individualized and humanistic approach.

Outside of my time spent tutoring, I am very interested in farming and animal husbandry and have spent two years off and on working on goat and cow dairy farms. I also enjoy reading philosophy, hiking, biking, and practicing martial arts. I look forward to meeting you!

Teaching philosophy

I strongly believe in demystifying the admissions process and helping each student present a unique story about themselves through their admissions materials. At the heart of my process is a rejection of a ready-made approach and an emphasis on the questions that make college meaningful in the first place: Who are you, and who do you want to become?

My method is personalized and dialogic. Through active conversation with the students, I push them to identify and crystallize a narrative about themselves that represents a strong case for admission. What are they passionate about? What’s important to them? What values do they believe in, and how do they want to actualize them in the world? An effective approach to college admissions writing is built on a core of authentic reflection on the part of the student, and I view my role as facilitating that.

I work with my students on developing the writing craft tools they need to write powerful admissions essays. Together, we go through how to ‘show, not tell’ through the aggregation of concrete, specific, and vivid details. But these skills aren’t just instrumental. I’m a big believer in the value of writing as a tool of self-reflection and a mechanism for thinking.

Overall, I believe that getting into your dream school isn’t about having perfect grades or world-changing achievements on your résumé. Those things can help, but what really matters is your ability to give the reader, for a moment, a glimpse into your inner world and the things that truly matter to you. The best admissions essays are successful exercises in empathy, and my goal is to help my students achieve that.
