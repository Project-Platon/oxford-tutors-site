Ricardo Feliciano Rivera

Educator & Tutor · STEM, Language & Whole-Child Learning

A Cum Laude chemist and educator whose Montessori, Socratic, and project-based approach builds curiosity and confidence across the sciences, languages, and the skills beneath them.

Biography

Ricardo Feliciano Rivera is an experienced educator and tutor passionate about fostering curiosity and confidence in his students. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry, graduating Cum Laude from the University of Puerto Rico, Mayagüez Campus, and an Advanced Master’s in Innovation and Educative Transformation from the Universidad Complutense de Madrid. He has worked in a variety of educational roles, including tutor, mentor, classroom assistant, and project manager across Puerto Rico, Spain, and the United States.

His teaching expertise spans Chemistry, Math, Reading, Writing, Science, Spanish as a Second Language, Executive Functions, and Social and Emotional Learning. He is committed to adapting his approach to meet each learner’s needs, drawing from Montessori, Socratic, and project-based methodologies. Much of his experience comes from working as a soccer coach on teams spanning all ages, including both men’s and women’s teams. He is certified in Adult and Pediatric First Aid/CPR/AED and holds a Preschool Child Development Associate (CDA) credential, and he recently obtained his Pediatric Disaster Response and Emergency Preparedness Training.

Ricardo believes in cultivating a supportive, empathetic, and engaging learning environment. He strives to connect academic material to students’ interests and to encourage learning through exploration and discussion. Outside the classroom, he is deeply involved in youth soccer, having served as a coach and organizer, and enjoys bringing lessons from the field into his teaching. Families can expect a dedicated, thoughtful, and encouraging partner in their child’s academic journey.

Teaching philosophy

My teaching philosophy is rooted in the belief that education should inspire curiosity, foster critical thinking, and empower students to embrace the learning process. Drawing from Montessori, Socratic, and project-based methodologies—and informed by my experience as both an educator and a coach—I aim to create an environment where students feel safe to explore, question, and learn by doing.

I believe that students thrive when they are encouraged to connect new concepts to their interests and prior knowledge. By bridging theory and practice, I help students see the relevance of what they are learning, making the material memorable and meaningful. I strive to make learning a collaborative journey, where mistakes are viewed as natural and valuable steps toward growth rather than setbacks. My goal is to nurture resilience and a positive mindset, so students develop confidence in their abilities and pride in their progress.

Building strong, trusting relationships is central to my approach. I prioritize establishing an environment that is peaceful, empathetic, and free of judgment or ego. By setting myself on equal footing with my learners, I open space for authentic communication and mutual respect. This foundation helps students feel heard and understood, paving the way for effective teaching and mentoring.

I also place great importance on modeling soft skills and emotional intelligence. I strive to be a mentor who listens empathetically, supports students’ personal growth, and celebrates their unique perspectives. Families can expect a tutor who is dedicated not only to academic excellence but also to the holistic development of each learner, equipping them with the tools to succeed both in and out of the classroom.
