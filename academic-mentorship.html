Overview

Oxford Tutors pairs students with mentors who help them think more clearly, write more precisely, and approach academic work with confidence. Sessions are built around the student’s mind, goals, school context, and long-term trajectory.

This is the long-term core of most partnerships: sustained, one-on-one intellectual engagement in the Oxford tutorial tradition, rather than episodic tutoring.

What it includes

  • Writing & humanities
  • Math & sciences
  • AP, IB & honors coursework
  • Study habits & metacognition
  • Long-term mentor relationships
